description

Al-Maghtas, also known as Bethany Beyond the Jordan, is a Christian pilgrimage and archaeological site on the Jordan River’s eastern bank. Churches, baptismal remains and other structures preserve the religious history of a landscape associated in Christian tradition with the baptism of Jesus. Visitor routes link archaeological interpretation with places of pilgrimage. Access is managed through a controlled entrance, so organise the visit with the site rather than attempting to reach the river independently. Religious ceremonies and ordinary sightseeing may require different arrangements.

Amman Roman Theatre
المدرج الروماني في عمّان

description

The Roman Theatre is one of downtown Amman’s most recognisable monuments. Its stone seating climbs into the hillside, illustrating the scale of public entertainment in the Roman city. The theatre dates to the second century CE and was designed for thousands of spectators. A visit combines close observation of the structure with the atmosphere of the surrounding city. Nearby heritage museums can add context when open, but their ticket and access arrangements should be checked. Choose how far to climb according to your comfort with the steep seating steps.

name

Aqaba Marine Reserve
محمية العقبة البحرية

description

Aqaba Marine Reserve protects a Red Sea landscape of coral reefs, seagrass and deeper reef habitats. UNESCO inscribed the property in 2026, recognising its ecological importance. It is a managed conservation area, not a single beach with one universal visitor entrance. Understanding the reserve means respecting the different purposes of its marine zones. Snorkelling or diving must be planned through authorised access points and suitable licensed services, with current rules confirmed before entering the water. An offshore heritage reference point is useful for understanding the area but must never be used as driving directions to a beach.
تحمي محمية العقبة البحرية مشهداً من الشعاب المرجانية ومروج الأعشاب البحرية وموائل شعابية أعمق في البحر الأحمر. وقد أدرجتها اليونسكو عام 2026 تقديراً لأهميتها البيئية. وهي منطقة حفظ منظمة، وليست شاطئاً واحداً له مدخل موحد لجميع الزوار. يبدأ فهم المحمية باحترام الأغراض المختلفة لمناطقها البحرية. ويجب ترتيب السباحة بقناع الغطس أو الغوص عبر نقاط دخول معتمدة وخدمات مرخصة مناسبة، مع تأكيد القواعد الحالية قبل النزول إلى الماء. وتفيد النقطة التراثية البحرية في تحديد المنطقة عموماً، لكنها ليست اتجاهات قيادة إلى شاطئ.

name

Azraq Wetland Reserve
محمية الأزرق المائية

description

Azraq Wetland Reserve protects an oasis ecosystem in Jordan’s eastern desert. Established by RSCN in 1978, it is an important place to understand wetland conservation and the movements of migratory birds. Water, vegetation and desert surroundings create a landscape unlike the country’s highland reserves. Use the approved wetland paths and observation points that are open during your visit. A slow, quiet approach gives more room for observing habitat and learning about restoration. Bird numbers and species vary; neither a particular sighting nor access to every route can be guaranteed.

name

Jerash Archaeological City
مدينة جرش الأثرية

description

Jerash offers a broad archaeological walk through the streets and public spaces of an ancient city. The Oval Plaza, colonnades, theatres and temples help visitors understand how monumental buildings related to everyday urban life. Hadrian’s Arch forms another distinctive part of the archaeological landscape.
