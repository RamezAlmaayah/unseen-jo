## Grounding and design decisions

- Actual app: React/Vite discovery service with Explore/map/destination detail, rule-based Surprise Me, preferences, profile, saved places, self-reported visits, and recommendation history.
- No implemented booking, ticketing, payment, travel-agency or user-to-destination contracting capability was found. A saved destination is not a reservation.
- No public UGC review/comment/submission feature. Optional avatar upload and profile information are described narrowly, with only the permission needed for that feature; no ownership transfer or broad marketing license.
- Administrative CMS is privileged; ordinary registration grants no administrator access. Terms do not change guards or roles.
- Destination pricing, timing, routes, opening/access details and prototype data are qualified. Approximate map points and straight-line distances are not navigation/safety guarantees. Visitors should verify details with the operator or authoritative source.
- Asset display does not establish ownership. Destination/media licenses, credits and public image reuse rights require an owner audit; do not claim that every image is owned by Unseen Jo.
- No automatic moderation/suspension/appeal or deletion service is invented. Signing out is not deletion.
- No sweeping liability waiver, invented damages cap, mandatory arbitration, exclusive foreign venue, or waiver of non-excludable consumer remedies.
- No invented minimum age, company domicile, governing-law choice, effective public-launch date, address or legal email. Applicable mandatory law and Jordanian context are acknowledged; final contractual law/jurisdiction needs counsel and operator facts.
- No forced registration checkbox or versioned terms-acceptance record was introduced. Links are accessible before the submit button and open a new tab to preserve entered form values. The current implementation does not claim to record consent.
