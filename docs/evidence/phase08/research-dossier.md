## Method and evidence tiers

- **V — Officially verifiable:** historical/site description supported by Jordan Tourism Board, UNESCO, RSCN or official Petra authority; source URL attached.
- **P — Published operational figure:** fee or schedule reproduced from an official public page, but must be checked for freshness and applicability to the exact ticket category before live publication.
- **U — Unverified/needs operator check:** entry coordinates, current hours, specific transit accessibility, actual opening today, facilities, or photographic reuse rights where unavailable from consulted sources.
- **E — Editorial estimate:** duration/suitability or recommended season can be added only as a *clearly identified estimate*, never as an official fact.

**Critical rule:** “No empty-looking cards” is a UI requirement, **not permission to invent data**. Continue researching unknown operational items; for genuinely unpublished values, display useful confirmed alternatives (official booking/contact link or 'Confirm with operator'), and do not claim the unknown was verified.

## Editorial quality gate for every new place

**Required core fields** (otherwise draft): Arabic and English canonical names, unique slug, governorate/city or locality, well-supported 2–4-paragraph bilingual descriptions, historical/natural significance, category mapping to existing live taxonomy, distinct activities, weather-independent explanation of best visitor experience, source URLs with checked dates, geographic coordinates *with accuracy level and distinction between attraction centre and gate*, valid cover image with verified reuse rights, alt text EN/AR, image attribution, published-only eligibility and moderation/audit metadata.

**Operator-dependent fields** (research repeatedly; never fake): ticket price by residency/nationality and effective date; discounts and Jordan Pass; opening hours by season; last entry; closures; guided-tour/booking needs; on-site transport, parking, accessibility and accessible routes; family suitability; child policies; transport mode feasibility; permits/safety warnings. Keep fields unknown if unverified and display a clean context-appropriate visitor message.

**Recommendation metadata:** confirmed categories/interests; visit-duration estimate labelled editorial; budget scoring only from a sufficiently documented cost model; transport score only from supported access data; group suitability limited to evidenced visitor facilities.

**Images:** A destination webpage image is NOT automatically reusable. Verify file-level license or obtain project-owned photos / written permission. Store photographer, original file URL, license/version, attribution and image identity. Prefer verified Commons pages (see [reuse requirements](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons:Reusing_content_outside_Wikimedia)); never use Google `imgres` as a rights source. This research pass does not authorize or supply any photo for upload.

**Publishing:** First stage researched drafts, then authorized evidence-checked batch publication under a genuine Admin session. Do not bypass RLS, rights checks, or fake human approvals. Audit all state transitions. Freeze Phase 09/Trip Planner until MVP QA/launch decisions are made.
