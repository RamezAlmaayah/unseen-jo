## Geographic verification method

The five JTB map targets were followed from their named sections and read from the `!3d` / `!4d` target coordinates, not the offset `@` map camera position. As-Salt uses UNESCO property coordinates converted from DMS. Actual MapLibre renders of all six values were inspected in `docs/screenshots/content-completion/*-map.png`; marker context agrees with reserve/urban/area locations. This is geographic consistency, not surveying or confirmation of safe arrival. All six remain `approximate_center`; administrators must resolve entrance/arrival suitability before recording a location review.

Ajloun reserve and UNESCO biosphere-area reference points refer to different extents. Iraq Al-Amir's area pin is distinct from Qasr El-Abd; do not silently move the area record to a photograph's camera location. Dana's repeated activity reference pin does not identify every trailhead. Mujib's Adventure Center area pin does not locate the entire reserve.

## Conflicts and unavailable evidence

- Dana biological-zone and species totals differ between JTB, current RSCN and older UNESCO submission; numeric ecological totals were omitted.
- The official government reserve fee table and RSCN 2026 trail rates differ; retain both citations, prefer the dated operator package for that specific activity, and confirm payable totals. Numeric database price fields remain NULL.
- Umm Qais seasonal and generic Jordan Pass hours disagree. No fixed schedule was inserted.
- Mujib age/typical-season guidance comes from an explicitly dated 2025 operator PDF; it requires current confirmation. The 2026 price list does not prove a trail is open today.
- The supplied `docs/CONTENT_PUBLISHING_WORKFLOW.md` was absent at task start. A workflow document was created describing existing operations, without changing policy or schema.
- No systematic step-free accessibility audit, current transport timetable or blanket group suitability was established. These remain unknown, rather than copied from the demo.
